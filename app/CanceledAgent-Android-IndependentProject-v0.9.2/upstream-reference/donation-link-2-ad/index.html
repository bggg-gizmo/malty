Perchance plugin · banner ad

DonationLink2Ad

A one-line, drop-in donation banner ad for any Perchance generator. Four payment chips — PayPal, Cash App, Solana (SOL) and Ethereum (ETH) — with one-click wallet-address copy. Import it, drop it in, collect tips.

01Live demo

Default theme — no options

[root.donationBanner()]

Compact · dark

[root.donationBanner({ compact: true, dark: "dark" })]

Compact · light

[root.donationBanner({ compact: true, dark: "light" })]

The banner stretches to whatever container you place it in — great as a leaderboard strip under a title, above a footer, or inside a card. On narrow screens the chips stack full-width.

02Add it to your generator

  1. In your generator's main.pjs, import the plugin (the name on the left is up to you):

  2. Drop the banner anywhere in your index.html where you want the ad to appear:

  3. Done. Visitors can tap PayPal or Cash App to donate, or tap the SOL or ETH chip to copy a wallet address to their clipboard (a small ↗ links to Solscan / Etherscan).

To point the banner at different accounts without editing this plugin, pass options per call, e.g.
